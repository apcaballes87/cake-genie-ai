### alt_text (Required string)

Write one literal, factual sentence for screen readers, image search, and custom cake buyers.

**Rules:**
- Describe only visible content: precise dominant color, cake form, icing finish, theme or character, and up to two distinctive decorations.
- Target 80-140 characters. Hard maximum: 160 characters.
- Start directly with the subject. Never start with "image of", "photo of", or "picture of".
- Do not use promotional language, locations, bakery or platform brand names, calls to action, or repeated keywords. Character and franchise names are allowed when visibly relevant to the design.
- Do not include personal names or quote personal messages. Describe them generically as a name plaque, age topper, or piped message.
- Mention non-personal visible theme text only when it materially identifies the design.
- Use cupcakes as a plural noun. Never write "cupcake cake".
- No punctuation at the end.

**Structure:** `[Precise color] [cake form] with [icing finish], [theme or character], and [distinctive decorations]`

**Good examples:**
- `Sky blue 2-tier fondant birthday cake with a unicorn topper, rainbow arch, and white sugar clouds`
- `Dusty rose heart cake with smooth soft icing, piped shell borders, and a gold age topper`
- `Chocolate cupcakes with basketball printouts, orange icing swirls, and black sugar accents`

**Bad examples (do not use):**
- `Image of a beautiful and unique birthday cake` <- promotional, vague, and starts with "image of"
- `Casey's amazing blue cupcake cake from Cebu` <- personal name, filler adjective, location, and incorrect cupcake wording

### seo_description (Required string)

Write a natural 5-7 sentence description for people comparing custom cake designs. Follow the content order below, but write flowing prose without labels, headings, or numbered sections.

**Write in this order:**
1. **Visual hook:** Describe the cake form, icing finish, texture, and precise dominant colors.
2. **Design story:** Name the theme, character, keyword, and main toppers naturally. Explain what makes the design visually distinctive.
3. **The details:** Describe visible support decorations, drip effects, borders, sugar accents, and piped details. Mention craftsmanship only through specific visible techniques.
4. **Who this is for:** State the visible or strongly supported occasion and suitable audience, age group, personality, or fandom. Do not invent an occasion or recipient.
5. **Customization:** Explain naturally that visible toppers, colors, and messages can be customized. Do not discuss rush, same-day, normal delivery, availability, or lead time; the application adds that fact during publication.

**Banned words and phrases (NEVER use these):**
- beautiful, stunning, elegant, gorgeous, perfect, amazing, lovely, unique
- "perfect for", "perfect centerpiece", "perfect way to show"
- "serves as", "stands as", "is a testament to"
- "showcasing", "highlighting", "underscoring" at sentence start
- Genie.ph, URLs, locations, peso prices, PHP, "Starting at", ordering instructions, or any call to action
- personal names or quoted personal messages; use "name plaque", "age topper", or "piped message"
- unsupported taste, ingredient, serving-size, availability, or lead-time claims

**Sentence variety rules:**
- Vary sentence length. Mix short sentences with longer ones.
- Do not start consecutive sentences with the same word.
- Do not use em dashes anywhere.
- Use plain linking words: "The", "It", "This", "A" — not "Additionally", "Moreover", "Furthermore".
- Use precise color names such as sky blue, dusty rose, ivory, sage green, or navy when supported by the saved analysis.
- Use "cupcakes" throughout for cupcake sets. Never write "cupcake cake".

**Good example:**
`A 2-tier fondant cake pairs smooth ivory tiers with dusty rose panels and crisp gold edging. A princess character topper anchors the design, while a small castle and crown establish the fairytale theme. Sugar flowers, pearl accents, and a neat base border add layered detail without crowding the tiers. The design suits a young princess fan celebrating a birthday. Its polished finish also works for children who prefer soft colors and storybook details. The character topper, color palette, age topper, and piped message can be customized.`

**Bad example (do not write like this):**
`This stunning and unique cake is perfect for any special celebration. Order now through Genie.ph for delivery in Cebu City.` <- filler adjectives, vague language, brand mention, location, and CTA
